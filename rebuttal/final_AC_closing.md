**On the narrowing of claims.** The meta-review notes that careful narrowing could change the assessment. We narrowed in four places rather than defended: the guarantee is now stated on the empirical calibration-set gap, with the population form separated; the diagnostic scope is stated as input-conditioned; GSM8K-style structural gaps remain a stated limitation rather than a solved case; and, following Reviewer eQL6's follow-up, PRISM's attribution is explicitly coordinate-relative.

**What changed through the exchange with Reviewer eQL6.** Reviewer eQL6 showed that a functionally invariant reparameterization of the proxy representation and head can change the axis attribution. We agreed: PRISM does not define a coordinate-free split over that equivalence class. The exchange established where the boundary lies, and we have committed to state it in the main text: the axes are measured relative to the stored parameterization and the stated alignment, and are not invariants of the input-output function; the evaluated PTQ and frozen-head LoRA settings satisfy the inherited-coordinate condition by construction; attribution after joint backbone-head optimization, including full SFT/RLHF, is outside the validated scope. A formal orthogonal-equivariance remark and the corresponding limitation enter the revision, and the sentence describing the split as identifiable from the bound's decomposition is replaced. Theorem 1 itself is unaffected: it holds for every stored model and every stated orthogonal alignment. We are grateful for a follow-up that made the framework's scope precise.

If the five points are judged met, we hope that can be reflected in the recommendation. If any is still judged partial, several hours remain and we would welcome the chance to complete it. Thank you for a review process that has concretely improved the paper.
