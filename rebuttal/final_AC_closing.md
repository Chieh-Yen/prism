Title 欄:Scope Clarification Following the Reviewer Discussion

The evidence for the five requests in the meta-review is already indexed in the Point A to Point E comments and the summary comment of 30 July, and in the per-reviewer summaries of 2 August, so we will not repeat it here. One substantive scope clarification emerged afterwards.

Reviewer eQL6's counterexample showed that PRISM's axis attribution is not invariant over the full $GL(d)$ equivalence class: functionally equivalent parameterizations can receive different scale, shape, and head diagnoses. We agree, and we will make this explicit in the main text. The clarification bears on how the attribution is interpreted, not on Theorem 1 or on any reported result: the bound remains valid for each stored model and specified $W\in O(d)$. No component is required to remain frozen. Our evaluated comparisons use a shared inherited parameterization, and the reviewer likewise noted that this is "likely not an issue in the post-training variants studied in the paper". Joint backbone-head optimization, by contrast, does not guarantee preservation of that convention, and comparing attributions across such checkpoints would require an additional canonicalization criterion. The revision states the convention, adds the exact $O(d)$-equivariance boundary, and replaces the earlier "identifiable from the bound's decomposition" wording.

This is the fourth place where the discussion led us to narrow the claims rather than defend them, alongside stating the guarantee on the empirical calibration-set gap, scoping the diagnostic as input-conditioned, and keeping GSM8K-style structural gaps as a stated limitation. We thank Reviewer eQL6 for exposing this boundary, and the Area Chair and reviewers for a discussion that materially strengthened both the evidence and the scope of the paper.
