Title 欄:What PRISM Adds at the Same Cost, and Where We Stop Claiming

Thank you for going back through the paper and the discussion before the window closes. It gives us the chance to be precise about what the framework adds and where our validation stops.

**What PRISM is for.** It is not a replacement for generative evaluation or for an NLL screen. The measured cross-entropy gap comes from the very pass PRISM runs, and Theorem 1 bounds that same gap; what the pass additionally yields is a decomposition of it, into a scale term that is a norm ratio, a shape term that is an alignment residual, and a head term that is a readout mismatch. Against generation the saving is real: 8.9 s against 556.7 s of greedy decoding on identical prompts, 62.6x. Against an NLL screen there is no saving, and the revision will report the measured gap beside the bound at no additional cost, without claiming a ranking advantage we have not measured. A scalar gap does not localise the drift, and because the shape term is differentiable, the quantity diagnosed is also the quantity trained: as a penalty it leads EWC, L2-SP, replay and layer-freezing at matched plasticity.

**Joint updates, and why full SFT is future work.** The ambiguity requires the compensating pair $(Z_P,H_P)\mapsto(Z_PA,A^{-1}H_P)$; modifying both the backbone and the head is not sufficient for it. The evaluated GGUF configurations make the point: they quantize both the backbone and `lm_head`, tensor by tensor in the inherited coordinates, and the attribution is unaffected. Under joint gradient optimization nothing anchors the inherited convention, and that is precisely why full SFT and RLHF are listed as future work rather than claimed. The line is not rhetorical: it is where our validation stops.

**On task data.** For an evaluation, having validation text is close to a free assumption: if a variant can be assessed at all, that text exists, and PRISM needs only a teacher-forced pass over it rather than a generative run. The calibration we report is leave-one-out within a setting, as stated. What we do not claim is generic-text use for the diagnostic: that is unpaired use and is stated as out of scope. As a fallback datum, single-seed: a generic WikiText reference of 19 sequences still reduced forgetting, to 0.773 against 0.724 to 0.736 with the task's own reference. We report it as an observation, not a result.

**On the workflow.** We accept that broad reliability is not established. What is established: the head case closes the loop end to end, with 75.77 of 76.95 attributed to the head term and the targeted fix verified at a 20x lower bound; single-axis interventions move only their own term (at least 2.6e5x selectivity, bound holds 26/26); and the rollout-conditioned test reaches +0.947 against +0.958 teacher-forced. Broader generation settings remain open, and the revision scopes the workflow claim to the settings in which it was validated.

Thank you again for the care in this final reading.
